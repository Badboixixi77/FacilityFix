## 🎯 Recruitment Strategy

### **Sourcing Channels**
- **Technical Roles:** GitHub, Stack Overflow, AngelList, LinkedIn Recruiter
- **Executive Roles:** Executive search firms, networking, industry events
- **Sales/Customer Success:** LinkedIn, industry associations, referrals
- **Junior Roles:** University recruiting, bootcamps, internship programs

### **Hiring Process**
1. **Screening Call (30 min)** - Initial fit assessment
2. **Technical Assessment** - Coding challenge or case study
3. **Technical Interview (60 min)** - Deep technical dive
4. **Culture Fit Interview (45 min)** - Team alignment
5. **Executive Interview (30 min)** - Senior roles only
6. **Reference Checks** - Professional references
7. **Offer & Negotiation** - Compensation discussion

### **Onboarding Process**
- **Week 1:** Company orientation, tool setup, team introductions
- **Week 2:** Product training, architecture overview, development environment
- **Week 3:** First tasks, mentor pairing, code review process
- **Week 4:** Full integration, project assignment, goal setting

## 🏢 Organizational Culture

### **Core Values**
1. **Customer First** - Every decision prioritizes customer success
2. **Technical Excellence** - High standards for code quality and architecture
3. **Continuous Learning** - Investment in team growth and development
4. **Transparency** - Open communication and information sharing
5. **Accountability** - Ownership and responsibility for outcomes

### **Team Practices**
- **Daily Standups** - 15-minute team syncs
- **Weekly Sprints** - 2-week development cycles
- **Monthly Planning** - Roadmap and goal setting
- **Quarterly Reviews** - Performance and strategy reviews
- **Annual Planning** - Strategic planning and budgeting

### **Communication Tools**
- **Slack** - Daily communication
- **Jira/Linear** - Project management
- **Confluence/Notion** - Documentation
- **GitHub/GitLab** - Code management
- **Zoom** - Video conferencing

## 📊 Performance Metrics

### **Engineering Metrics**
- **Velocity:** Story points completed per sprint
- **Quality:** Bug rate and resolution time
- **Code Coverage:** Test coverage percentage
- **Deployment Frequency:** Number of deployments per week
- **Lead Time:** Time from feature start to deployment

### **Product Metrics**
- **Feature Adoption:** Usage of new features
- **User Satisfaction:** NPS and user feedback
- **Market Fit:** Customer retention and expansion
- **Innovation:** New features shipped per quarter

### **Business Metrics**
- **Revenue Growth:** MRR and ARR growth
- **Customer Acquisition:** CAC and LTV
- **Customer Success:** Retention rates and NPS
